Privacy policy

Basic Memory rclone is a personal tool operated by its owner to read and copy Markdown notes between the owner's VPS and Google Drive.

After authorization, rclone accesses Google Drive data as needed for commands run by the owner. Google may grant the application permission to view, create, change, and delete Drive files. The rclone configuration specifies the Basic Memory folder as its working root; that setting does not restrict the permissions granted by Google.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the rclone configuration on the VPS. Notes are stored on the owner's VPS and Google Drive. The owner controls any further sharing and can revoke the application's access through their Google Account settings.
